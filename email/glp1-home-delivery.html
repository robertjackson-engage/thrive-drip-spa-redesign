Your medication is coming home.
Beginning October 15, all GLP-1 medications transition to home delivery — fewer trips to the spa, the same ThrIVe care.

Dear ThrIVe GLP-1 Clients,

We are excited to share an upcoming enhancement to our GLP-1 Weight Loss Program designed to make your treatment more convenient, flexible, and easier to incorporate into your everyday life.

Instead of coming into the spa regularly to receive your GLP injection, you will be able to have your GLP-1 medication shipped directly to your home, generally in a three-month supply. This means fewer trips to the spa, greater flexibility, and the convenience of continuing your weight-loss treatment from the comfort of your home.

While the way you receive your medication is changing, our commitment to you is not. ThrIVe will continue to be your partner throughout your health and weight-loss journey.

Your ongoing support
What the updated program includes
01 In-spa weigh-in every three months
These help us follow your progress, answer questions, and continue supporting your goals. Our nurses will take your most recent biometric readings to provide for your telehealth call.
02 Telehealth Good Faith Exam follow-up with a Nurse Practitioner
To ensure appropriate clinical oversight and continuity of care. After your weigh-in, during your every-three-month visit to the spa, you will be connected to an experienced provider via a link sent to your phone. They will review any concerns or questions you have and help determine the next dose if a change is needed or requested.
03 Medication delivered directly to your home
Generally, a three-month supply will be called in to our partnering registered 503A pharmacy. You can also elect to have your three-month supply sent to the spa for pickup at your earliest convenience. Our knowledgeable nurses will walk you through the process of self-injection.
04 Blood work every six months
Regular lab monitoring gives you and your healthcare team valuable information about what is happening inside your body throughout your journey. These results help us follow important health markers over time, identify meaningful changes, and provide another way to measure your progress beyond pounds lost.
Important
Updated lab work required before October 15

To help prevent any delay in treatment during this transition, all clients who have not completed blood work within the past three months will need updated labs at their next check-in, before October 15. We encourage you to plan for this as soon as possible.

Fasting is encouraged, as we will be checking lipid levels along with other important health markers.

Required labs: CBC · CMP · Lipid Panel · Amylase · Lipase · Hemoglobin A1c · TSH · T3 · T4

If your most recent labs with ThrIVe were completed less than nine months ago, you may be approved for a one-month supply to allow additional time to complete the required lab work and avoid interruption in treatment.

Labs are accepted from other providers if obtained within the last 4–6 weeks.
