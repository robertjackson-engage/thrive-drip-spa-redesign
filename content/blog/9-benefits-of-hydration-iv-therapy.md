Drinking water is a surprisingly inefficient way to hydrate; IV hydration puts fluids where the body needs them. Nine reasons to book a [Hydrate Drip](../hydrate-drip.html):

1. **Efficiency.** Throat and digestive tissues absorb most of what you drink, whereas IV fluids enter the bloodstream directly.
2. **More than fluids.** Intense workouts deplete electrolytes and nutrients that water cannot replace; a drip replenishes both immediately.
3. **Easier on digestion.** Large volumes of fluid strain the gut and can flush nutrients; IV delivery avoids the digestive system and wastes less.
4. **Faster recovery.** After workouts, long office days or a hangover, rapid fluids and vitamins restore energy and reduce soreness.
5. **Customization.** Formulas target athletic recovery, energy, hangover, skin care or immunity.
6. **Immunity.** Vitamin C and B12 delivered intravenously are more effective than oral doses.
7. **Toxin flushing.** Dehydration burdens the liver and kidneys; proper fluids help them filter and eliminate toxins.
8. **Cognition.** The brain slows at the first signs of dehydration and is among the last tissues to rehydrate by drinking; IV hydration restores focus and memory faster.
9. **Skin, joint and muscle health.** Dry skin, joint pain and aches signal inadequate hydration, and a rapid influx of fluids and nutrients restores them.
