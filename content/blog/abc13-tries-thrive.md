When ThrIVe opened as the first IV therapy drip spa in Texas, Houston's ABC13 wanted to see what the fuss was about. Reporter Natasha Barrett booked the [Party Drip](../party-drip.html) — a liter of fluids with anti-inflammatory and anti-nausea medication — and filmed the whole visit.

The segment, "New drip spa offers IV treatment for ailments," walked viewers through the lounge, the nurse-led process and the menu, from hangover recovery to immunity and beauty drips.

You can watch the original story on ABC13's site: [abc13.com/health/iv-vitamin-drip-therapy](https://abc13.com/health/iv-vitamin-drip-therapy/1337468/).

Since then, ThrIVe has grown to ten lounges across Houston, Dallas and Fort Worth, plus a mobile concierge service. The Party Drip is still one of the most-booked drips on the menu — [try it yourself](../book.html).
