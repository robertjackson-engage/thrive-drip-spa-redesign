The Myers' cocktail is one of ThrIVe's most popular treatments, named for the doctor who gave the first IV vitamin treatments in the 1970s.

## What's in a Myers Drip

- **Vitamin C** — immune support.
- **Vitamin B12** — red blood cell and nerve health, less fatigue.
- **B-complex** — eight B vitamins for cellular health, brain function and stress.
- **Electrolytes** — nerve, muscle and hydration balance.
- **Magnesium** — fatigue, headaches, migraines, muscle tension, anxiety and sleep.

All delivered with one liter of saline. Optional boosts: **glutathione** (antioxidant, immune support) and **zinc** (immune, digestive, skin and eye health).

## Four kinds of benefit

**Hydration.** Up to 75% of Americans are chronically dehydrated, and water is essential for absorbing nutrients. The IV delivers fluid the body absorbs quickly.

**Energy.** Chronic fatigue links to dehydration, stress, nutrition and vitamin deficiency. B vitamins support cellular energy and magnesium eases fatigue — and it outlasts coffee or energy drinks.

**Pain.** Magnesium relaxes muscles and supports nerve health, easing cramps, headaches and tension.

**Overall wellness.** Many clients use it proactively to keep body systems in balance.

## Why IV beats oral

IV delivery bypasses digestion, where oral nutrients can be lost or absorbed inconsistently, giving faster and more consistent results.
