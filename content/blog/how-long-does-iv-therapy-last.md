Duration depends on the therapy, your metabolism and your overall health. Because IV therapy delivers fluids, vitamins, minerals and nutrients directly into the bloodstream, absorption is immediate rather than delayed by digestion.

## By component

- **Fluids and electrolytes: 2–3 days**, depending on activity and hydration habits.
- **Vitamins: 2–3 weeks.** Water-soluble vitamin C and B-complex are used within about 24 hours and the excess excreted; fat-soluble vitamin D can linger for weeks.
- **Minerals such as magnesium and zinc: 1–2 weeks.** Felt within hours and stored for later use.
- **Antioxidants like glutathione: 1–3 days** of active presence, though benefits such as reduced inflammation may last longer.

## What shortens or extends the effect

A fast metabolism, your hydration status, the type of therapy (hydration is short-term; immunity or energy formulas last longer), lifestyle habits like diet, exercise and sleep, and your health goals — chronic issues may need regular sessions.

## How often should you come?

Monthly for general wellness. Weekly or biweekly for specific concerns such as chronic fatigue or immune support, guided by a healthcare professional. [Memberships](../memberships.html) make a weekly or monthly rhythm easy and cheaper.

## Even after the nutrients clear

Better hydration supports energy and skin for days, antioxidants and vitamins strengthen immunity over time, and recovery support aids long-term performance. Regular sessions help maintain consistent benefits.
