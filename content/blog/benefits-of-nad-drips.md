NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a coenzyme present in every cell, and in foods such as turkey, chicken, whole milk, oatmeal, broccoli and cabbage. It regulates metabolism, repairs cells and manages inflammation — but levels fall with age and are hard to replace through diet, leading to low energy, brain fog and metabolic challenges. An NAD+ drip delivers a concentrated dose directly.

## The benefits

- **Cellular health** — activates repair enzymes (sirtuins and PARPs) and protects cells from oxidative stress.
- **Energy** — helps convert nutrients into ATP, which is why it's popular with athletes, first responders, military personnel and people with demanding jobs.
- **Metabolism** — improves processing of glucose, fat and protein, hormonal balance and insulin sensitivity.
- **Mental clarity** — protects neurons and supports focus, memory, mood and reduced brain fog.
- **Anti-aging** — strengthens skin cells against free radicals and helps inflammatory conditions like acne and rosacea.
- **Recovery** — accelerates muscle recovery, reduces inflammation from overtraining, and supports detoxification by restoring brain chemistry and easing withdrawal symptoms.
