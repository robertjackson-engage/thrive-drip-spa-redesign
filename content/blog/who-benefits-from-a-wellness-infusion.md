IV drips suit many lifestyles, and a Good Faith Exam at the first visit confirms suitability. Here's who benefits most, and which drip to start with.

## Athletes

Athletes lose electrolytes through sweat; deficits cause fatigue, cramping and dizziness and raise injury risk — even moderate dehydration slows brain function. Many NFL players use pregame IV hyperhydration, mainly for cramping. Start with the [Hydrate Drip](../hydrate-drip.html) (one liter of fluids plus electrolytes) or the [Fit Drip](../fit-drip.html), which adds amino acids for output and recovery. Professional athletes should check anti-doping rules first.

## Frequent travelers

Jet lag costs roughly a day of recovery per time zone crossed, with insomnia, poor judgment, nausea and headaches. The [Migraine Drip](../migraine-drip.html) targets headaches; [NAD+](../nad.html) supports clarity and balance.

## Beauty and wellness enthusiasts

Results from the inside out: the [Detox Drip](../detox-drip.html) clears toxins for better skin, the [Slim Drip](../slim-drip.html) boosts metabolism before an event, and the [Beauty Drip](../beauty-drip.html) is the anti-aging option.

## Nutrition seekers

With research pointing to declining nutrient quality in food, the [Myers Drip](../myers-drip.html) — electrolytes, vitamin C, B12, B-complex, magnesium, with optional zinc and glutathione — fills the gaps.

## On the mend

Recovering from illness, or living with weakened immunity from conditions or medications? The [High C Drip](../high-c-drip.html) carries our highest vitamin C dose; the [Immuno Drip](../immuno-drip.html) adds glutathione and zinc.

Consult your healthcare provider; a licensed provider screens every client before treatment.
