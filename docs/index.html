01 The first IV drip spa in Texas

Health, innovated.

ThrIVe Drip Spa was founded in Houston as the first IV therapy drip spa in Texas. We took treatments that have lived in hospitals for decades — a liter of fluids, vitamins straight to the bloodstream — and moved them into a lounge with reclining chairs, blankets and a nurse who knows your name.

Led by MIT graduate Dr. Eugene Lou, our drips are formulated by physicians and pharmacists and administered by registered nurses after a Good Faith Exam. Rehydrate, restore and recharge — then get on with your day.

Our expert advice

All FAQs →

IV vitamin therapy delivers fluids and essential nutrients directly into the bloodstream for maximum absorption. Up to 85% of a vitamin pill can be lost to digestion; an IV delivers close to 100%. It helps combat dehydration, boost energy, support immunity and enhance overall wellness.

Relaxing. You'll settle into a reclining lounge chair while a registered nurse places your IV and builds your drip. Complimentary beverages, protein bars and cozy blankets are part of the visit. Most drips take 30–45 minutes; NAD+ runs longer.

Every drip is administered by experienced registered nurses following a Good Faith Exam with a licensed provider. Our spa consultants walk you through the menu and help you choose. We're LegitScript certified.

Dehydration, fatigue, immune support, hangovers, migraine relief, athletic recovery, skin and beauty goals, and more. If you're not sure which drip fits, our nurses will build one for you.

Fluids and nutrients reach the bloodstream immediately. Many clients feel the difference before they leave the chair; the effects of a Myers or Beauty drip can last for weeks.
