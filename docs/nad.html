01 What is NAD+

Your boost for balance and vitality

NAD+ IV therapy delivers the coenzyme straight to the bloodstream, where it powers mitochondrial energy production, DNA repair and cellular signaling. Clients report improved energy, sharper mental clarity and focus, and a foundation for healthy aging.

Infusions run longer than a standard drip — plan on up to two hours in the lounge. Prefer something quicker? NAD+ shots take minutes, and an NAD+ booster can be added to any drip.

04 Good to know

NAD+ questions

All FAQs →

NAD+ is a coenzyme found in every cell. It drives energy production in the mitochondria, supports DNA repair and regulates cellular signaling. Levels naturally decline with age; IV delivery restores them directly.

Most clients feel warmth or mild pressure as the infusion runs; our nurses slow the rate to keep you comfortable. Plan on up to two hours — bring a book or a laptop.

Clients looking for more energy, sharper focus, help with stress and recovery, and support for healthy aging. Ask our consultants which dose fits your goals.

Shots are quick and affordable. Drips deliver a larger dose over time. Memberships (500–1,500 mg per month) are the most cost-effective way to stay consistent; loading doses front-load a reset over 2–5 days.
