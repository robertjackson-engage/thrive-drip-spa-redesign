06 Hydration solutions

Rehydration, explained

All FAQs →

Water makes up about two-thirds of the body. Common causes of dehydration include sports and exercise, heat, illness, travel, work stress, and alcohol and caffeine. Even mild dehydration shows up as fatigue, headache, brain fog and dry skin.

Up to 85% of a vitamin pill can be lost in digestion — only about 15% reaches your bloodstream. An IV delivers fluids and nutrients directly, so you feel the difference quickly.

Even 2% dehydration affects skin, organs and cognition. Every ThrIVe drip includes at least one liter of IV fluids with electrolytes, and you can add an extra half liter for maximum hydration.
