How Long Does IV Therapy Stay in Your System?

Fluids for days, vitamins for weeks, minerals in between — how long each part of a drip works, and how often to come back.

Book a Drip →
ThrIVe Drip Spa

IV therapy has become a popular treatment for a variety of health needs, from boosting energy and hydration to supporting the immune system and aiding recovery. If you have considered IV therapy, you may wonder: how long does IV therapy stay in your system? The answer depends on several factors, including the type of IV therapy, your body's metabolism and your overall health. This article takes a detailed look at how IV therapy works, how long its effects last, and what influences its duration in your body.

What is IV therapy?

IV therapy, or intravenous therapy, delivers fluids, vitamins, minerals and other nutrients directly into your bloodstream through an IV drip. Unlike oral supplements, which must pass through the digestive system, IV therapy allows for immediate absorption, so the fluids and nutrients are available to your body right away.

How long does IV therapy stay in your system?

The duration of IV therapy's effects varies with the specific nutrients used, your body's needs and your lifestyle. Here is a breakdown of the common components of an IV drip and how long they typically stay in your system.

Fluids and electrolytes: 2–3 days

IV fluids help rehydrate your body and restore electrolyte balance. These effects can last for a few days, depending on your activity level, hydration habits and overall health.

Vitamins: 2–3 weeks

Water-soluble vitamins like vitamin C and B-complex are absorbed quickly and used by the body within about 24 hours. Excess vitamins are excreted through urine. Fat-soluble vitamins, like vitamin D, can stay in your system longer, sometimes up to a few weeks.

Minerals: 1–2 weeks

Minerals such as magnesium and zinc are stored in your body and used as needed. While their immediate effects can be felt within hours, the benefits may extend for one to two weeks.

Antioxidants such as glutathione: 1–3 days

Antioxidants like glutathione help combat oxidative stress. While their active presence diminishes within a few days, their benefits may last longer.

Factors that influence how long IV therapy lasts

Several factors affect how long IV therapy remains in your system and how long you feel its effects:

  • Your metabolism. People with faster metabolisms may process nutrients more quickly, reducing the duration of their effects.
  • Your hydration status. Staying hydrated helps maintain the benefits of IV therapy. Dehydration may shorten the effects of the fluids and electrolytes.
  • The type of IV therapy. IV therapy for hydration may have immediate but short-term effects, while drips built around immunity or energy may offer longer-lasting benefits.
  • Your lifestyle. Diet, exercise and sleep quality influence how your body uses the nutrients from IV therapy.
  • Your health goals. For ongoing wellness goals, regular IV therapy sessions may be needed to maintain consistent benefits.

How often should you get IV therapy?

The frequency of IV therapy depends on your individual health needs and goals. For general wellness, many people benefit from monthly sessions. Those addressing specific concerns, such as ongoing fatigue or immune support, may choose weekly or biweekly treatments. A healthcare professional can help you determine the right frequency based on your needs. Memberships make a weekly or monthly rhythm easier to keep.

Benefits of IV therapy over time

While the nutrients in IV therapy do not remain in your system indefinitely, their effects often extend beyond their physical presence. For example:

  • Increased hydration. Proper hydration supports your body's natural processes, helping energy levels and skin health for days after treatment.
  • Immune support. Antioxidants and vitamins support your body's natural defenses over time.
  • Improved recovery. IV therapy supports muscle recovery after exercise and long-term physical performance.

Understanding the duration of IV therapy's effects

The nutrients delivered through IV therapy remain in your system for varying lengths of time, depending on the type of treatment and your body's unique needs. Fluids and electrolytes work right away, while vitamins and antioxidants offer benefits that can last for weeks. Regular IV therapy sessions can help you maintain consistent wellness and reach your health goals.
