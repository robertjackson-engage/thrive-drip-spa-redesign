01 IV drip therapy

About the drips

IV therapy administers vitamins, minerals, antioxidants and amino acids directly into the bloodstream through intravenous infusion. It bypasses the digestive system and has been clinically shown to be more effective than oral supplements.

Our treatments are administered by experienced registered nurses using safe, effective procedures, after a Good Faith Exam with a licensed provider. Our spa consultants guide you through the process and build a personalized plan.

Celebrities and athletes made it visible, but the medical community has used IV therapy for decades to safely treat and manage a range of conditions and help people improve overall health.

Every client is different, but our goal is one 'stick'. Our registered nurses use lidocaine to numb the site for each treatment (if you have no allergy).

The benefits are felt with a single treatment. On average our clients visit one to two times per week, depending on their goals.

Drip times vary by formula and by client; plan a little extra time on your first visit for the Good Faith Exam. NAD+ infusions take up to two hours. Shots take minutes.

Fluids and electrolytes 2–3 days; vitamins 2–3 weeks (water-soluble C and B used within a day); minerals 1–2 weeks; antioxidants like glutathione 1–3 days. Effects on energy, skin and immunity often last longer.
